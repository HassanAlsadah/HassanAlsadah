### About me

I'm a software engineer moving into digital product management. I like turning a messy problem into a clear product decision, then shipping it.
I studied Software Engineering (Cloud Computing) at KFUPM, interned as a software engineer at Halliburton in Singapore, and completed Olo Academy's Digital Product Management bootcamp.
I'm PSPO I and CAPM certified, and I work in Arabic and English.

### What I bring

- **Product thinking:** discovery, competitor research, PRDs, prioritization, roadmaps and metrics, written down and reasoned through.
- **Technical depth:** I can talk to engineers about trade-offs, and ship a working product myself.
- **Delivery discipline:** Scrum (PSPO I) and project management (CAPM).

### Featured project: [Dreamplish](https://github.com/HassanAlsadah/Dreamplish)

<a href="https://github.com/HassanAlsadah/Dreamplish">
  <img src="assets/dreamplish.png" alt="Dreamplish: From the dream to the offer" width="100%" />
</a>

**Problem:** job seekers track applications in scattered places and spend hours researching each company before interviews.
**Solution:** a job tracker that writes a sourced company briefing for every application, checks your fit, tailors your CV and preps you for interviews, in Arabic and English.
**Process:** discovery → competitor analysis → PRD → MoSCoW/RICE → shipped live → roadmap and metrics.

[Case study](https://github.com/HassanAlsadah/Dreamplish/tree/main/docs/product) · [Live demo](https://dreamplish.vercel.app) · [Decisions log](https://github.com/HassanAlsadah/Dreamplish/blob/main/docs/product/08-decisions-log.md)
